1

The real problem

This is not bad luck. The ranking and indexing slips trace back to a few specific, fixable things in how the stores are set up and how their URLs get indexed. The symptoms are easy to see. What matters is the cause, and that is what this covers.

2

What is actually happening

Index clutter and migration leftovers

Duplicate and tracking-parameter URLs, plus a few old pages still live alongside the new structure. This can waste crawl budget and push your real pages further down the results.

On-page basics left empty

Empty main headings, homepage titles that read as just the domain name on two stores, and missing page descriptions. Small gaps that quietly cost rankings and click-through.

Tracking and measurement gaps

Missing tracking pixels and uneven tag setup. Without clean tracking you cannot see where buyers drop off, which turns any conversion work into guesswork.

Page weight

Home and product pages carrying heavy scripts and images. On mobile this slows the load, and slow pages quietly lose sales.

What is already working

Your product pages and their structured data are solid. This is a snapshot, not a scare. The fixes are focused and prioritized, not a rebuild.
